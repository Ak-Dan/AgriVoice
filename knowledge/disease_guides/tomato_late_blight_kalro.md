## Symptoms

Tomato late blight can cause dark, water-soaked leaf lesions, stem lesions, and fruit damage. It spreads quickly in cool, wet, humid conditions.

## Farmer-Safe Guidance

- Scout tomatoes frequently during wet weather.
- Remove and destroy heavily infected plant material where local guidance permits.
- Avoid overhead irrigation and improve airflow around plants.
- Do not compost infected material unless safe composting guidance is available.
- Ask an extension worker about locally recommended fungicide timing and safe use.

## Prevention

Use clean seedlings, resistant varieties where available, staking or spacing for airflow, crop rotation, and sanitation.

## Escalation

Escalate when disease spreads quickly, appears after prolonged wet conditions, or affects fruits and stems.
