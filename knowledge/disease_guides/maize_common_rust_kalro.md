## Symptoms

Common rust appears as raised red-brown pustules on maize leaves, often on both leaf surfaces. Older pustules may darken as spores are released.

## Farmer-Safe Guidance

- Plant clean seed from a reliable source.
- Use tolerant or resistant maize varieties where available.
- Bury or remove infected crop residue after harvest to reduce carry-over.
- Improve airflow by avoiding overly dense stands and removing heavily infected lower leaves where practical.
- Rotate maize with non-host crops such as legumes where suitable.

## Prevention

Use clean seed, tolerant varieties, residue management, and rotation. Chemical control should be based on local extension advice and disease severity.

## Escalation

Escalate when rust appears early, spreads quickly, or affects many plants before grain filling.
