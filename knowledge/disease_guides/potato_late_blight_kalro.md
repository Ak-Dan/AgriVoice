## Symptoms

Late blight can spread quickly under cool, wet conditions. It affects leaves and stems and may lead to rapid crop damage if not managed early.

## Farmer-Safe Guidance

- Scout fields frequently during wet or humid weather.
- Remove volunteer potatoes and infected crop debris where practical.
- Use healthy seed tubers from trusted sources.
- Avoid overhead irrigation where possible.
- Seek local extension advice quickly when blight is suspected because timing of control is critical.

## Prevention

Use clean seed, resistant or tolerant varieties where available, good field sanitation, and timely local blight management recommendations.

## Escalation

Escalate when symptoms appear during wet weather, spread rapidly, or occur before tubers are well formed.
