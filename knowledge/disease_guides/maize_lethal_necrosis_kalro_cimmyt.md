## Symptoms

Maize lethal necrosis can cause chlorotic mottling, yellowing, necrosis, stunting, poor ear formation, and plant death. Symptoms may look like severe nutrient stress or other maize diseases, so confirmation is important.

## Why It Matters

MLN is a serious maize disease reported in East Africa. It involves Maize chlorotic mottle virus together with other maize viruses and can cause severe losses.

## Farmer-Safe Guidance

- Plant certified seed from trusted suppliers.
- Avoid recycling seed from affected fields.
- Remove heavily affected plants where local extension guidance recommends it.
- Control volunteer maize and grassy weeds that may host vectors.
- Rotate with non-cereal crops where possible.
- Report suspected MLN to extension services because it can affect wider communities.

## Prevention

Community awareness, clean seed, surveillance, resistant or tolerant varieties where available, vector management, and crop rotation are important.

## Escalation

Escalate immediately if many plants show severe yellowing, necrosis, stunting, or rapid field-level spread.
