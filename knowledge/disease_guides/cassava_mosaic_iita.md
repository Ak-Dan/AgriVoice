## Symptoms

Cassava mosaic disease usually appears as yellow-green mosaic patterns, leaf distortion, reduced leaf size, and poor plant growth. Severe infection can reduce root yield.

## Why It Matters

CMD is one of the most important viral diseases of cassava in Africa. It spreads through infected cuttings and by whiteflies.

## Farmer-Safe Guidance

- Plant clean, disease-free cassava stems.
- Use resistant or tolerant varieties recommended for the local area.
- Remove severely affected plants early when extension advice supports roguing.
- Do not share planting material from symptomatic plants.
- Control volunteer cassava plants and avoid keeping diseased planting stock.

## Prevention

The strongest control is clean planting material plus resistant varieties. Whitefly management and community-level sanitation help reduce spread.

## Escalation

Escalate if many young plants show mosaic symptoms, if symptoms appear soon after planting, or if a farmer wants to distribute cassava stems.
