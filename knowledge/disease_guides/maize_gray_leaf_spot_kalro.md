## Symptoms

Gray leaf spot causes rectangular gray to tan lesions on maize leaves. Lesions may follow leaf veins and can reduce photosynthesis when infection is severe.

## Farmer-Safe Guidance

- Plant clean certified seed.
- Scout fields regularly for early disease signs.
- Use tolerant varieties recommended locally.
- Remove, bury, or plough in infected residues; do not use infected stover as mulch.
- Weed regularly because some weeds can host pathogens.
- Avoid overhead irrigation where possible because splashing water can spread disease.
- Rotate away from maize for at least two seasons where land allows.

## Prevention

Clean seed, tolerant varieties, crop rotation, residue management, good fertility, and reduced leaf wetness are core practices.

## Escalation

Escalate when lesions spread quickly, appear before tasseling, or cover many leaves.
