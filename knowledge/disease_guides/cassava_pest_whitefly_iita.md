## Symptoms And Risk

Whiteflies can damage cassava leaves and also act as vectors for important cassava virus diseases. Heavy pest pressure can reduce crop growth and increase disease spread.

## Farmer-Safe Guidance

- Inspect the underside of cassava leaves for whiteflies and other pests.
- Avoid using planting stems from pest-infested or diseased plants.
- Keep fields clean and remove heavily affected planting material when advised.
- Encourage natural enemies by avoiding unnecessary broad insecticide use.
- Ask an extension worker before applying chemicals, especially when virus symptoms are present.

## Prevention

Use clean planting material, resistant varieties where available, regular scouting, and integrated pest management. Pest control alone will not cure virus-infected plants.

## Escalation

Escalate when whitefly numbers are high, virus symptoms are spreading, or nearby farms report cassava mosaic or brown streak symptoms.
